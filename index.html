4 Analysis screening tools
Planning dates:

Buildings, trees and terrain cast shadows (1 m LIDAR surface). BRE guidance looks for ≥ 2 h of sun on 21 March over half of an amenity space.

Indicative model: urban roughness, wakes behind tall buildings and street channelling. Not CFD or a wind-tunnel study.

Water spreads from rivers/water on the site to connected lower ground. Indicative extent only, not a flood-risk assessment.

data.police.uk street-level crime. Locations are anonymised to street points, so this shows where reports are attributed, not exact incidents or rates.

A route model on the street/path network from entrances (OSM, else inferred) to shops and stops. Likely activity, not counted footfall.
